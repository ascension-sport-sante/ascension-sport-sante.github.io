FAQ

Before you start

How does the assessment work?

It’s the first step of your coaching. I come to your home: a one-to-one interview (your health, your habits, what you want), an assessment of your abilities (balance, strength, flexibility, stamina), then we set your goals together. With Aube, we cover the essentials in 20 minutes; with Zénith, it’s complete, then repeated at 6 and 12 weeks.

I’ve never exercised, or I feel too old: is this for me?

Yes. We start exactly where you are, and each session adapts to how you feel that day. I coach everyone from children to grandparents, including after years without moving.

I have back pain, or a health condition: is it risky?

Adapted physical activity is precisely my job. I take your pain, your treatments and your doctor’s advice into account, and I adapt every exercise. If in doubt, we ask for their go-ahead before starting.

My child doesn’t like sport: can it work?

That’s often where it works best. Everything happens through play: challenges, obstacle courses, balls, ropes. They move without realising it, gain confidence, and the desire comes back at their own pace.

Do I need equipment, or a lot of space?

No. I bring the equipment: resistance bands, mats, small accessories. A corner of your living room, a balcony or your garden is enough.

Where do you travel to?

All of Paris, from the 18th, and the inner suburbs: Aubervilliers, Boulogne, Clichy, Issy, Ivry, La Défense, Montreuil, Montrouge, Neuilly, Pantin, Saint-Denis, Saint-Ouen and Vincennes. Your town isn’t listed? Just ask me.

How does the tax credit work?

Home sessions are eligible for the French 50% tax credit for home services (services à la personne), for French tax residents, once my declaration is registered (it is in progress). With the Urssaf immediate advance (avance immédiate: the public service that advances your tax credit), you only pay your half, directly: no need to wait for the following year’s tax return.

Can my health insurance reimburse it?

Some French top-up health insurers (mutuelles) cover part of adapted physical activity sessions, especially on medical prescription (exercise on prescription, “sport sur ordonnance”). Check with yours: I provide the invoices you need.

When will I see results?

It depends on your starting point, your commitment, and your goal. We repeat the assessment tests regularly: you see, with figures to back it up, what has changed.

How many sessions do I need?

It depends on your goal: we talk about it at the first session. You can come session by session, with no commitment (Éclat), or follow Zénith, a 12-week programme with commitment, which is cheaper, with your progress measured at 6 and 12 weeks.

Zénith: what if I have to stop before the end?

For a legitimate reason (lasting inability to exercise, hospitalisation, moving out of my area, job loss or an unexpected drop in income), you stop with no notice and no fees. Otherwise, you can stop at any time with 2 weeks’ notice; a session that didn’t take place is never charged. A temporary health problem? We take a break, and the plan is extended by the same amount of time.

What if I don’t like the first session?

You get your money back. That’s the idea behind Aube, the first session: you try it with no risk.

What if I need to cancel?

Let me know at least 24 hours before: we move the session, free of charge. Less than 24 hours before, or if you don’t show up, the session is due, unless there is a legitimate reason (illness, emergency, hospitalisation, unforeseeable circumstances). Ill that very morning? Call me: it isn’t due.

I pay for my mother’s sessions: who is entitled to the tax credit?

The tax credit also covers services provided “at the home of one of your ascendants” (a parent or grandparent), if they receive the “personalised autonomy allowance (APA)”. It cannot be combined with deducting maintenance payments made to that parent. If your parent pays for their own sessions at home, they are the one who benefits. Source: service-public.gouv.fr, fact sheet F12 (our translation), checked on 15 April 2026.
